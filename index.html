The Abbey of Death — A Story-Driven First-Person Shooter

Clutching stolen German troop-movement charts, English spy Alistair Finch flees across the Free France border onto a sheer 100-meter limestone outcrop. Inside the ruined abbey waits Abbot Henri, the last monk alive with a hidden British wireless. Hold room by room as ammunition runs dry—until the French Resistance scales the cliff face and British paratroopers race the 48-hour clock to dawn.
